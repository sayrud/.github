Sayrud is an open-source, self-hosted collaborative spreadsheet database for organizing projects, tasks, and shared data. Bring your team together around one table, choose the view that fits your work, and deploy it on your own infrastructure.

## Make shared data work for your team

- **One table, four views.** Switch between grid, kanban, gallery, and form views. Each view has its own filters, sorting, grouping, and visible fields.
- **Work together in real time.** See online teammates and the cells they are editing. Copy and paste cells, edit records in batches, and undo or redo changes.
- **Fields for more than text.** Use numbers, dates, single-select and multi-select options, checkboxes, attachments, and formulas alongside text.
- **Extend your workflow.** Connect through the REST API and [OpenAPI specification](https://github.com/sayrud/sayrud/blob/HEAD/docs/swagger.yaml). Admins can publish JavaScript field shortcuts that regenerate when referenced fields change.
- **Fit your team's environment.** Configure OAuth2, OIDC, or SAML single sign-on, or LDAP authentication. Choose from ten interface languages and light, dark, or system themes.

## Get started

Try the [live demo](https://try.sayrud.com/) to explore Sayrud in your browser.

Visit [sayrud/sayrud](https://github.com/sayrud/sayrud) for the application source and setup instructions. The [deployment guide](https://github.com/sayrud/sayrud#-deployment) walks you through running Sayrud, PostgreSQL, and Redis with Docker Compose.

Explore the [documentation](https://docs.sayrud.com/) and [blog](https://docs.sayrud.com/blog) for more about the project.
